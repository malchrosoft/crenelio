# Tradesperson's checklist: quotes, parts, supporting documents

*Translation of the French version dated 01/11/2026 — the French version prevails. Official texts are quoted in French.*

*Last check: 01/11/2026. Summary of the sheets [devis-depannage](devis-depannage.md),
[retractation](retractation.md), [debours](debours.md) and [note-facture](note-facture.md).*

## Before the job

- A quote **from the first euro**, given before any work, stating whether it is free or charged.
- Non-urgent job: leave the customer their 14-day withdrawal period, or obtain their express request to
  start earlier.
- Genuine emergency (danger to people or premises): limit yourself to what is **strictly necessary**;
  everything else is covered by a separate quote, subject to withdrawal.

## Parts bought for the customer (disbursements, *débours*)

- Obtain the customer's **mandate** (*mandat*: type of parts, maximum budget) before buying.
- Have the invoice or receipt made out **in the customer's name** (name, address; for a business: SIRET,
  intra-EU VAT number). A receipt in the tradesperson's name turns the part into a resale.
- **Keep the originals** of invoices and till receipts: they justify the amounts excluded from turnover in
  case of an audit (URSSAF, tax authorities).
- Give the customer a **copy** of the supporting document with the invoice.
- Re-invoice the **exact amount** of the supporting document, without margin, on a separate line; above the
  mandate budget, ask for a new agreement.
- Record « débours » in the **receipts book** (*livre des recettes*); do not declare it as turnover.
- Never fuel, mileage costs or tools as disbursements.

## After the job

- Give the **note / invoice** (mandatory above €25 incl. VAT) **before being paid**.
- Offer the replaced parts; if the customer does not keep them, have them sign the **release** (*décharge*).
- Keep accepted quotes, mandates and supporting documents (retention period: Item 39).
