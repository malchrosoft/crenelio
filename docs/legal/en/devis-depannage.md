# Quotes for repair, maintenance and servicing at home

*Translation of the French version dated 01/11/2026 — the French version prevails. Official texts are quoted in French.*

*Last check: 01/11/2026. Sources: order (*arrêté*) of 24 January 2017; DGCCRF sheet « Dépannage à
domicile » (2018), see [sources](../sources.md).*

## Who is concerned

Troubleshooting, repair, maintenance, replacement and addition of parts in buildings (masonry, heating
and air conditioning, chimney sweeping, insulation, joinery, locksmithing, roofing, plumbing, sanitary
installations, waterproofing, plastering, painting, glazing, coverings, electricity, rainwater and
wastewater, unblocking) and in home equipment (electrical, electronic, household appliances, alarms,
pest control…). The quote is due **from the first euro**.

Not concerned: connections to a public network by a concession holder, and services covered by a flat
fee paid when the contract is concluded.

## Name

The text speaks of a **pre-contractual document** (*document précontractuel*); Crénelio calls it
**« Devis »** (quote). Once accepted, it becomes the contract.

## Mandatory content (DGCCRF sheet, based on the order)

> « Lors de l'intervention, le professionnel est tenu de vous communiquer, préalablement à la
> réalisation de tous travaux, un document précontractuel détaillé qui contient les informations
> suivantes :
> - la date de rédaction ;
> - le nom et l'adresse de l'entreprise ;
> - le nom du client ;
> - le lieu d'exécution de l'opération ;
> - la nature exacte des réparations à effectuer ;
> - le décompte détaillé, en quantité et en prix, de chaque prestation et produit nécessaire à
>   l'opération prévue : dénomination, prix unitaire et désignation de l'unité à laquelle il s'applique
>   (notamment l'heure de main d'œuvre, le mètre linéaire ou le mètre carré) et la quantité prévue ;
> - le cas échéant, les frais de déplacement ;
> - la somme globale à payer hors taxes et toutes taxes comprises, en précisant le taux de T.V.A. ;
> - la durée de validité de l'offre ;
> - l'indication du caractère payant ou gratuit du document précontractuel. »

*Explanation (not an official translation):* before any work, the professional must give a detailed
document with: date; company name and address; customer name; place of work; exact nature of the
repairs; detailed breakdown of each service and product (name, unit price, unit such as labour hour,
linear or square metre, and quantity); travel costs if any; total excluding and including tax with the
VAT rate; validity period of the offer; whether the document is free or charged.

For a contract concluded **off-premises** (*hors établissement*, at the customer's home) or at a distance, in addition:

> « l'existence et modalités d'exercice du droit de rétractation. »

*Explanation (not an official translation):* the existence of the right of withdrawal (*droit de
rétractation*) and how to exercise it.

See [retractation.md](retractation.md) and, for the company's identity,
[mentions-entreprise.md](mentions-entreprise.md).

## Official text (checked on 04/11/2026)

Order of 24 January 2017, consolidated version: [textes/arretes.md](../fr/textes/arretes.md). Details:

- **Before the contract** (art. 2): hourly labour rates **including VAT**, **method of counting the
  estimated time**, flat-rate prices including VAT, travel costs, whether the quote is charged or free
  **and its cost**, any other remuneration condition; this information must be **easily accessible on any
  online communication space** of the professional (art. 3): the showcase website (*vitrine*) is concerned.
- **Off-premises quote** (art. 4, II): it takes the form of the contract of articles L221-5 and L221-9 of
  the Code de la consommation (French Consumer Code), with hourly rate and **estimated time** (or flat
  rate) for each service, products with unit price, unit and quantity, travel costs.
- **Replaced parts** (art. 4, III and annex 2): standard model, word for word:
  « Souhaitez-vous conserver les pièces, éléments ou appareils remplacés ? OUI □ NON □ — Signature du
  consommateur précédée de la mention manuscrite " Lu et approuvé ". »
  *Explanation (not an official translation):* "Do you wish to keep the replaced parts, items or
  appliances? YES □ NO □ — Consumer's signature preceded by the handwritten words 'Read and approved'."
- **Note** (art. 5): see [note-facture.md](note-facture.md).

**In Crénelio**: the date or period of performance and the payment terms (C. consom. L221-5, 3°;
R221-2, 4°) are entered on each quote and are required to send it; the estimated time count (art. 2) is
printed under the labour; the company's intra-EU VAT number (R111-2, 5°) appears next to the SIRET.

## Replaced parts

The order (art. 4, III and annex 2) requires informing the customer that they may **keep the replaced
parts, items or appliances**, using a "yes / no" form preceded by « Lu et approuvé » (read and approved).
The DGCCRF sheet states:

> « Pour les pièces remplacées, le client a le droit de conserver les pièces ou appareils remplacés. Au
> cas où il refuse de les garder, le prestataire doit lui faire signer une décharge. »

*Explanation (not an official translation):* the customer may keep the replaced parts or appliances; if
they refuse to keep them, the provider must have them sign a release (*décharge*).

**In Crénelio**: the customer answers when accepting the quote; a refusal is recorded as a release (date
and IP address).

## Penalties

The DGCCRF sheet indicates, for non-compliant advertising, a fine of up to €100,000; abuse of weakness
(*abus de faiblesse*: emergency situation, vulnerable customer) is a criminal offence (€375,000 fine,
prison).
