# Service note (invoice)

*Translation of the French version dated 01/11/2026 — the French version prevails. Official texts are quoted in French.*

*Last check: 01/11/2026. Source: DGCCRF sheet « Dépannage à domicile » (order (*arrêté*) no. 83-50/A of
3 October 1983 as amended), see [sources](../sources.md).*

> « Pour toute prestation d'un montant supérieur à 25 euros TTC, le professionnel est tenu de vous
> remettre un document sur lequel apparaissent :
> - les coordonnées du prestataire ;
> - la date de rédaction de la note ;
> - les dates et lieu d'exécution de la prestation ;
> - le décompte détaillé, en quantité et en prix, de chaque prestation et produit fourni ou vendu
>   (ex : taux horaire et nombre d'heures travaillées) ;
> - la somme totale à payer hors taxes et toutes taxes comprises ;
> - le nom du client, sauf opposition de celui-ci.
>
> La note doit être délivrée dès que la prestation a été rendue, et en tout état de cause avant le
> règlement du prix de la prestation. »

*Explanation (not an official translation):* for any service above €25 incl. VAT, the professional must
give a document showing: provider's details; date of the note; dates and place of the service; detailed
breakdown in quantity and price of each service and product supplied or sold (e.g. hourly rate and hours
worked); total excluding and including tax; customer's name, unless they object. The note must be given
as soon as the service is rendered, and in any case before payment.

Below €25 incl. VAT, the note is given at the customer's request.

## Official text (checked on 04/11/2026)

Order no. 83-50/A of 3 October 1983, art. 1 to 4, and order of 24 January 2017, art. 5:
[textes/arretes.md](../fr/textes/arretes.md). Details compared with the DGCCRF sheet:

- the note is mandatory when the price is **greater than or equal to €25 incl. VAT** (and not "above");
  below, at the customer's request; for home repair services, on request **whatever the amount**, given
  **free of charge on a durable medium**;
- the **detailed breakdown** becomes optional if the service was covered by a descriptive and detailed
  quote, accepted and consistent with the work performed;
- the note is drawn up **in duplicate**: the original to the customer, the copy kept **two years** by the
  provider, filed by date.

## Tax mentions on the invoice (checked on 04/11/2026)

CGI (Code général des impôts, French General Tax Code), annex II, art. 242 nonies A
([textes/code-general-impots-annexe-2.md](../fr/textes/code-general-impots-annexe-2.md)), version of decree
no. 2024-1195. For a tradesperson:

- full name, **identification number** (SIREN) and address **of the professional and of their customer** (1°);
- date of issue (6°) and **unique, chronological and continuous number** (7°);
- for each service: quantity, precise description, **unit price excluding tax** and VAT rate or exemption
  (8°); **nature of the transactions**: exclusively services, exclusively supplies of goods, or both (8° bis);
- date of the service if it differs from the date of issue (10°); total excluding tax and VAT per rate
  (11°); rebates and discounts (9°);
- under the basic VAT exemption scheme (*franchise en base*), mentions 2° (VAT number) and 12° (reference
  of the exemption) may be omitted (II), but the mention « TVA non applicable, article 293 B du CGI » (*VAT
  not applicable, article 293 B of the CGI*) remains required by article 293 E of the CGI.

Mentions 1° (customer number), 7° bis and 8° bis apply to invoices issued from 1 September 2026, and from
**1 September 2027** for micro-enterprises and SMEs (e-invoicing timetable, NOTA of the article). The
chronological and continuous number is managed by the tradesperson's invoicing software.

## In Crénelio

The invoice is drawn up in the tradesperson's tool (Item 22, part B); Crénelio does not issue it.

- **« Préparer la facture »** (Prepare the invoice) sheet (completed appointment): customer, address, date
  and place of the service, reference of the accepted quote, detailed lines (quantity, price, VAT), totals
  and exemption mention, each ready to copy; **disbursements** (*débours*) on a separate line, at the
  **exact amount of the supporting document** entered by the tradesperson with its reference, and an alert
  if the mandate budget is exceeded.
- **CSV exports**: customers (initial import) and completed jobs for a period, disbursements separately.
- Sheet: nature of the transactions (8° bis) to copy, reminders of mentions 1° and 7°.
- The tax mentions of the invoice (continuous numbering, VAT mentions, e-invoicing) are the responsibility
  of the tradesperson's software.
